Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

1. Data we access

When you authorize rnes with TikTok, we receive: your basic profile info (open ID, display name, avatar) via user.info.basic, and permission to upload and publish videos to your account via video.upload and video.publish. We also receive OAuth access and refresh tokens.

2. How we use it

Only to upload the videos you choose, apply the captions you write, publish them at the time you schedule, and show posting status. We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Storage and security

Tokens and scheduled-post information are stored on the operator's own server and are used only by the App. Video files are kept only as long as needed to complete the upload.

4. Retention and deletion

You can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok (Security and permissions → Apps and services). To have stored tokens and data deleted, contact us (see below); we delete them within 30 days.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

6. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

7. Contact

Open an issue at github.com/katenestk-art/rnes/issues
